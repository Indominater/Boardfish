Method and limits
  • Style comparisons use a blank 150 × 90 world-unit panel at 100%, 125%, and 200%, with explicit canvas DPR. Screenshot the CSS reference at matching browser DPR for direct native-pixel comparisons. CSS screenshot bounds are exposed by the harness.
  • Layer checks compare the exact same GPU renderer with the production visibility planner and a complete painter-order reference. Lower shadows must remain wherever a higher opaque surface does not cover them. Identical overlapping boxes therefore retain their visible outside shadows.
  • Tall-panel precision checks render a 2,400,000.25-unit panel and a 2,000.25-unit reference with their visible bottom edges placed at exactly the same device-pixel coordinates. Actual WebGL readbacks must match at device scales 0.697, 0.713, and 1.31; native PNG pairs include the bottom border, corners, and shadow.
  • The overlapping workload puts 999 small boxes, including their shadows, beneath a larger opaque top panel. The separated workload fits all 1,000 boxes in the viewport. Both paths receive 12 warmup and 90 measured frames by default.
  • CPU submission and requestAnimationFrame intervals are separate metrics. Timed frames contain no screenshot, pixel readback, GPU finish, or synchronization. These measurements do not directly measure GPU execution or confirmed presentation.
  • Native PNGs and numerical checks supplement visual inspection. Canvas2D, CSS, and GPU edge filters can differ slightly without indicating an ordering error.
